How good is your gut?

Before this module hands you the real numbers, find out what your instincts say. Eight questions about the scale, geography and money of global migration. Drag the slider to your honest guess — no grades, no tricks — then see how your answer compares with the best available data.

Research on public opinion finds that most people, in most countries, get these numbers strikingly wrong — usually in the same direction. By the end you'll see your own perception profile: where your instincts run high, where they run low, and why that matters for how migration gets debated.

Dots left of the line: you guessed low. Right of the line: you guessed high. The scale is logarithmic — the far edges mean a guess four times (or more) away from the data.

Why this matters. Migration debates are usually fought with feelings about numbers rather than the numbers themselves — and the gap between the two is where the politics happens. Module 2 is about learning to check the figure, ask who counted it, who was left out of the count, and how the same statistic can be framed as a crisis or a fact of life. Keep your profile in mind as you meet these numbers again in the lecture.
